In most units the month gets assembled twice: once as punches, and again at month end as a salary sheet someone types by hand. Two records of the same month always drift apart, and every argument at the pay counter starts in that gap.

Zyraa exists to close it.

## One record, read by everything

The punch, the shift rule, the approval and the payslip all read and write the same record. Payroll doesn't rebuild the month — it reads it. Nothing is typed twice, so nothing drifts.

## From project to product

Building the HRMS for one company was a project. Turning it into Zyraa meant a different set of questions:

- Can another unit set its own shift ladder without asking me?
- Can HR see *why* a day was judged the way it was?
- Can a department head approve from the floor, on a phone?

That's why Zyraa ships as three apps — an admin portal, an employee web app and a mobile app — on one system.

## What changed for me

Projects end at the demo. Products start there. The VINKORE team now takes Zyraa to more units, and every new unit brings a rule I hadn't thought of. That loop — listen, build, ship, fix — is the part I enjoy most.
